\-Project Details-

&#x09;Built in Java using custom Swing graphics, Our retro 2D side-scrolling shoot 'em up puts player in the cockpit of a starship battling through five intense waves of escalating alien waves and boss encounters. The gameplay involve dodging incoming barrages and complex enemy movement patterns while collecting speed and multi-shot power-ups to upgrade the ship's fire rate and maneuverability.



\-How to play and mechanisms-

Controls: Use the Arrow Keys to maneuver and dodge enemy bombs or incoming alien ships. Press the Spacebar to fire missiles.



Combat: A single missile strike destroys regular alien ships, while bosses possess high health pools requiring sustained hits.



Defeat Conditions: The game ends in a Game Over if:

1. Your ship collides with an enemy bomb or alien craft.
2. Any alien sneaks past your ship and reaches Earth.  



\-Alien behavior-

1. Alien1 is a standard, slow-moving enemy that advances steadily toward the player.
2. Alien2 moves in a swift zig-zag pattern, making it a difficult target to hit.
3. Alien3 charges up energy before executing high-speed sequential dash attacks directly at the player.
4. Boss1 is a massive enemy that moves vertically up and down while firing two parallel bombs simultaneously.
5. Boss2 is a high-HP boss moving in a fluid sine-wave pattern while discharging a 3-way bomb spread from its center.
